The models

BMT-X

18.06 €/MWh on 377 days

Boosting with multiple testing (Kapetanios, Sarafidis & Ventouri), the method from my thesis. One model per hour, with weather, holidays and gas.

LEAR-X

17.86 €/MWh on 377 days

Lasso on the last two years of prices and inputs, one model per hour. The standard benchmark in price forecasting papers.

XGBoost-X

18.14 €/MWh on 377 days

Gradient-boosted trees on the same inputs as LEAR-X.

R network

17.50 €/MWh on 377 days

A small network written in base R: two layers (192 and 96), dropout, Huber loss. Three seeds averaged, refit every day.

PyTorch network

16.49 €/MWh on 377 days

Two layers (256 and 128) predicting all 24 hours at once, trained from scratch every day on the same inputs as LEAR-X. Three seeds averaged.

PyTorch, warm start

16.93 €/MWh on 377 days

A network plus a straight linear path, kept from one day to the next and only updated with the new day instead of retrained.

PyTorch, distribution

17.26 €/MWh on 377 days

The warm-start network, but it forecasts a Johnson SU distribution for each hour instead of one number. Its median is used here.

Chronos-2

15.56 €/MWh on 377 days

Amazon's pretrained time series model, used as it is with no training. It sees twelve weeks of hourly prices, and for tomorrow the weather forecasts, holidays, weekday and gas price.

Released in October 2025, so its training data may overlap the first weeks here. The days after it are the clean test.

TabPFN

18.07 €/MWh on 377 days

A pretrained model for tables. Each day it gets the last year as rows (hour, prices 1, 2, 3 and 7 days before, neighbour prices, weather, gas) and predicts tomorrow's rows without training.

Chronos-2, prices only

20.76 €/MWh on 377 days

Chronos-2 on the price series alone, to see how much the weather and gas inputs add. Not a candidate.

Timestral

14.41 €/MWh on 377 days

Mistral's pretrained time series model, released 11 November 2026, used as it is with no training. Same inputs as Chronos-2.

Released after these days, so it may have seen them in training and is kept out of the mixes. Its live record from 13 November 2026 is the real test.

Rules

  1. The test days and the scoring were fixed before any model was tuned or run.
  2. Settings were chosen only on every 7th day from October 2024 to September 2025, then frozen.
  3. Each forecast uses only data available before the day-ahead auction closes. Nothing is refit after seeing the test days.
  4. Differences against today's ensemble are tested with Diebold-Mariano (Newey-West, 7 lags). The best mix is not trusted on its own: the tied set says how many mixes are as good.
  5. Winners go live as challengers, saved next to the forecast but not shown. Chronos-2 with the gas models, Chronos-2 with the PyTorch network, and Timestral with the gas models started on 13 November 2026. They get one early check on 15 December 2026 (p < 0.01), otherwise they are judged on 1 March 2027.
  6. A model released after the test days began may have seen them in training. It is shown, but kept out of the mixes, and only its live record counts.
